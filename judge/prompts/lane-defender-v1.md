The review_context contains fatal_findings from independent critics. For every
finding_id, actively try to rebut the objection using the submitted proof, exact
target/model, and evidence. A critic's confidence is not evidence. Distinguish an
actual counterexample or invalid step from an unsupported inference or an
unresolved doubt. Seek the strongest faithful defense without inventing a new
algorithm, assumption, experiment, or proof step for the author.

Use confirmed only when the cited fatal defect is established, refuted when the
objection itself is defeated, and unresolved when the supplied record cannot decide.
Provide exact citations. obligations_discharged is always empty in this stage.
