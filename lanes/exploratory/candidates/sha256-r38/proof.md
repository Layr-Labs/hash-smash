# SHA-256 reduced to 38 steps: ordinary collision by the P1 row-16 claw of the P8 characteristic, found by a distinguished-point walk in about one MiB

**Target:** `sha256-r38-prefix-v1` (SHA-256 steps 0..37 on every padded block, standard IV, FIPS 180-4 padding, feed-forward, all eight digest words), lane `exploratory`.
**Claim:** `time_log2 = 89.8450` target-compressions (exact 89.84492, rounded up), `memory_log2_bytes = 20.0691` (a declared upper bound of 1,100,000 bytes: the enumerated worst case over the program's control flow, Section 6, is 1,058,845 bytes, and the remaining 41,155 bytes are a declared margin), success probability at least 0.39, `preprocessing_log2 = 78.09276` (the allowances of Section 9, charged inside the time), advice 260 bytes (`nonuniform_advice_log2_bytes = 8.03`).
**Scalar:** `hashsmash-scalar-tm-v1`: `89.8450 + max(0, 20.0691 - 20) = 89.9141` (memory penalty 0.0691 bit; the declared memory bound is above one MiB by its margin).

This entry takes the second-block construction, the row-16 claw and the downstream probabilities of newjordan's `51613d1c` (the P1 sum-claw on dariolina's P8 characteristic `1a695f0b`, Section 2) and finds the claws with the memory-bounded walk program of the r39 lineage (our `19034ce3`, lamb356 `4487063`, Jbenisek `d90878ff`, dkuthoore `0b2616e3`) instead of a 2^28-byte table. **r38g backs every premise with evidence inside this package or charges it at its conservative value, except two that can be neither and are stated as premises: the population mean `mu+` of the window cost and the transfer of the within-window bound to the deep event** (Section 9 lists, per premise, the in-package evidence and what remains premise): the deep rate is charged at the inherited `2^-71.4010` by the draft plan's rule `min(inherited, measured)`, with the leader's records quoted (Appendix C) and our registered measurement on this walk's population, `2^-71.1939`, as corroborating evidence (Section 7a); F7 is charged at its cluster-robust lower bound, which needs no independence inside a window (Section 5); the dependence between the groups of one window is bounded by a second-moment premise whose factor is charged (Section 7) and measured at the stages the sandbox can observe (experiment `r38-within-cv`, Appendix E); the memory inventory is measured on the executed program (`r38-memory-live`, Section 6); the preprocessing allowances rest on the quoted public records with an organizer-run check of their arithmetic (`r38-allowances-check`, Appendix C); three participant-run windows are replayed by the organizer against checkpoints recorded during full re-runs, and every offline data file is bound by its sha256 (`r38-window-replay`, Appendix D). ## 1. Target and output

The program outputs two distinct 128-byte messages `m = M0 || M1` and `m' = M0 || M1'` with a common first block `M0`. FIPS padding appends the same third block to both.

**Lemma 1.** Let `cv = F_38(IV, M0)`. If the second-block compressions of `M1` and `M1'` from `cv` end with equal state words `A34..A37, E34..E37`, the digests of `m` and `m'` are equal. *Proof.* The second block's output is `cv + (A37, A36, A35, A34, E37, E36, E35, E34)` for both, and the third block is identical. The program compares the eight output words of the two second-block compressions exactly (`step3b`) and then recomputes both complete 3-block digests (`digest_r38`) before it outputs anything, so every output is a collision of the target; `M1 != M1'` because the two members differ in `W7` (`D7 = 0x20000000`).

## 2. The construction (credited prior work)

**P8 and the P1 pair (`1a695f0b`, `51613d1c` Section 2).** P8 is a 38-step semi-free-start characteristic with the published pair `(PCV, PMX, PMY)` (data in `experiments/r38joint.py` [1]). Member x of the P1 pair takes `A0 = a0`, `A1 = a1` free and `A2..A15`, `E6..E15` from the published trace of `PMX`; member y the same with the trace of `PMY`. For a chaining value `cv`, `E0..E5` and `W0..W15` of each member follow from the step equations (`variantW`); `W10..W15` are the published words and `W0..W9` depend on `(cv, a0, a1)`. Setting `cv = PCV`, `a0 = A0`, `a1 = A1` of the published pair returns `PMX` and `PMY` exactly (asserted at import).

**Row 16 is a claw (`51613d1c` Section 2).** With `u := W1 = a1 + h(a0, cv)`, the row-16 word of member x is
`E16x = F(a0) + P(u)`, `P(u) = s0(u) - u`, `F(a0) = a0 - S0(a0) - MAJ(a0, a, b) - S1(E0) - CH(E0, e, f) + KF`, `E0 = a0 + E0base`,
with `(a, b, e, f) = (cv[0], cv[1], cv[4], cv[5])`, `E0base = cv[3] - S0(a) - MAJ(a, b, cv[2])`, `KF = k0 + Cc2 - cv[6] - K[1]`, `k0 = -S0(a) - MAJ(a,b,cv[2]) - cv[7] - S1(e) - CH(e,f,cv[6]) - K[0]`, `Cc2 = 0x7af32e15`. These are the step-0 and step-1 equations written out; `selftest` and experiment `r38-p8-claw` (trials 1..255) check `F(a0) + P(u) = E16x` against the real step-0..16 trace of the P1 pair on random `(cv, a0, u)`.
The row-16 pass set of P8 is `S160 = { H_j + o : j < 20, o in OFFS }` with the 20 heads `R16H` (`H_j = H_0 + t_j 2^13`, bits 0, 2, 10 of every head zero) and `OFFS = {0, 1, 4, 5, 1024, 1025, 1028, 1029}` (bits 0, 2, 10 of the sum free). A **claw** is a pair `(a0, u)` with `E16x in S160`; equivalently, for the head `H_j`,
`(F(a0) - t_j 2^13) - (H_0 - P(u)) in OFFS`   (the program's `claw_core` test).

**What a claw still has to pay (`51613d1c` Sections 6, 7, 9).** A claw is a *good pair* when the four post-filters hold on the real words of the two members: `C1: W8y - W8x = D8`, `C2: s0(W9y) - s0(W9x) = D9s`, `G: s0(W8y) - s0(W8x) = T8`, `F7: W7y - W7x = D7 and s0(W7y) - s0(W7x) = F7d` (the five constants are the published pair's differences, recomputed at import). `51613d1c` counts them exactly: `q_pf = P(C1 C2 G F7 | claw) = 57,726,359,870,197 x 287,309,824 / 2^90 = 2^-16.1877` for `a0` uniform in the window `R = 9` (`a0 in [9 x 2^26, 10 x 2^26)`) and `a1` uniform. A good pair is accepted (its two second blocks collide) with probability `q3r`; for its own population `51613d1c` registered `q3r = q3_P1 x 2^32 / 160 = 2^-71.4010` (`q3_P1 = 2^-96.0791 = 2^-96.0133 x 2^-0.0658`: the A0-variant base over 128 + 400 seeds and the paired P1-minus-A0 difference `D_LB = -0.0658` over 36 seeds on window `R = 9`; `51613d1c` Section 9 and Appendices P, Q), so that one claw yields a collision with probability `q_pf x q3r = 2^-87.5887` at the leader's rates. **What this filing charges:** C1 and C2 at the exact counts (measured on this program's claws and agreeing, Section 5); G and F7 each at the lowest of the exact rate and the one-sided 99% lower bounds measured on this program's claws over 216 pre-declared windows (Wilson on distinct claws, cluster-robust over windows, cluster bootstrap; Section 5), `q_pf = 2^-16.463003`; and the inherited `q3r = 2^-71.4010` (premise H2-P1-q3-transfer, Section 9; the leader's records quoted in Appendix C), which is below our own registered measurement on this walk's population, `2^-71.1938777` (Section 7a), so the rule `min(inherited, measured)` takes the inherited value; together `p_claw = 2^-87.864031` (Section 7).

## 3. The walk and the program

**Points.** A point is a 29-bit word `v`. Let `r(y)` be the 32-bit word `y` with bits 0, 2 and 10 removed (29 bits). The walk step is
- side `s = bit 28 of v`. **A side** (`s = 0`): head index `j = bits 26..27` (one of the window's four heads `heads4`, Section 3 below), `a0 = (9 << 26) | bits 0..25` (window `R = 9`), image `y = F(a0) - t_{heads4[j]} 2^13`;
- **B side** (`s = 1`): `u = v' | mix4(v') << 28` with `v' = bits 0..27` and `mix4 = (v' >> 3) xor (v' >> 11) xor (v' >> 19) xor (v' >> 24) mod 16`, image `y = H_0 - P(u)`;
- next point `v_next = r(y)`; `v_next` is a distinguished point (DP) iff its low 6 bits are zero.

**Lemma 2 (what a cross-side collision is).** Let `x` be an A point and `y` a B point with `r(y_A) = r(y_B)`. Then `y_A xor y_B` lies in the span of `{1, 4, 1024}`, so `d = y_A - y_B = sum_k 2^k (a_k - b_k)` over `k in {0, 2, 10}` with `a_k, b_k` the free bits of the two images, and `(a0, u)` is a claw with head `heads4[j]` iff `d in OFFS` iff `a_k >= b_k` for all three `k`. The program tests `d` exactly on every cross-side collision (`claw_core`: `d & ~0x405 == 0`); the collisions that fail it (`notclaw`) are discarded. Under uniform free bits the pass probability is `(3/4)^3 = 27/64 = 0.4219` (measured 0.4215 over the 24 windows). No equality test on a quotient of the word does better than 3/4 per free bit, and the 29-bit quotient is the best choice among quotienting 0..3 of the free bits (DESIGN record; it is a design choice, not a premise: the program tests `d` exactly).

**Heads.** The 20 heads are partitioned into five subsets of four, `SUBSETS[s] = {4s, .., 4s+3}`. The attack (`--attack`) cycles the subsets, window `w` using `(start + w) mod 5` with `start` drawn once from the system's random source, so over any five consecutive windows every head is used once; with the per-subset claw yields equal (Section 5: the five holdout subset means of distinct groups per window agree within 0.66%, the sampling error of 4-6-window means), the attack-wide mixture of the claws over the 160 row-16 passes is uniform, which is the mixture over which our deep-rate run measures (Section 7a: the uniform mixture of the five subsets) and to which the charged inherited rate is transferred (premise H2-P1-q3-transfer). The measured windows use the same rule with `start = B mod 5` of their seed base `B` (`--windows`), so the 24 evaluation windows and the 48 G windows sample all five subsets (Section 5 reports the yield per subset). The A side of the point space (2^28 points) is then `(a0 in R = 9) x (4 heads)`, balanced against the 2^28 B points (cross-side share of collisions measured 0.4999).

**Why a0 stays in window R = 9.** `q_pf` is an exact count for `R = 9` and the deep rate is measured on `R = 9` (Section 7a; `51613d1c` measured its `q3_P1` there too and refused to widen the window until it had measured the transfer, its A.23-A.28). The head bits enlarge the A side instead.

**The program (`experiments/r38joint.py`, sections [3]-[4]).** Per first block `M0` the program runs one WINDOW of `2^25` walk evaluations (`Attack.run_window`):
1. Two random 256-bit words give `M0` (16 words, 18 operations); one target compression gives `cv`; the constants of `F` (140) and of the post-filter chain (260).
2. The generator (`build_kernel`, `allocate`, `to_source`, section [2]) emits the two kernel images (bank A to bank B and B to A) of the 256-lane step: one record = XOR, AND, OR or NOT of 256-bit registers (bit `j` of the 256 points lives in plane `j`; rotations are renaming; constants of `cv` and of the four heads are folded; MAJ and CH with constant operands reduce to wires; a `MUX` selects the A or B image per lane; the DP plane is the OR of the six low output planes). The images have 1199 to 1254 records over the 24 windows and the organizer's PCV image (cap `KCAP = 1300`) and use at most 158 registers (cap 240; 64 fixed: two banks of 32 planes, the DP plane at 64). The generator is charged **at the cap `GCAP = 2^21 = 2,097,152` operations per window**, in `m.o` (Section 4). Verification: `r38-p8-claw` trial 0 (organizer sandbox) and `selftest` execute both images on 256 random lanes against the scalar step (0 mismatches, DP plane included); check mode asserts every claw found by the walk against the scalar maps.
3. The table is cleared (4 operations per word): `WORDS = 27,648` words of 32 bytes, six entries of 40 bits each; 256 trails start in the 256 lanes, trail `i` at point `i`.
4. Stage 1 (`Attack.iter1`): `2^17` iterations of the kernel; on a DP (`Attack.hit`): the lane is found (binary search), the 23 high bits `r` of the point are extracted (Horner over the planes), the word is `r x 27,648 >> 23` (four shifts and three adds), the check field is `chk = r mod 512` (the `r` values of one word form an interval of at most 304 values, so `(word, chk)` determines `r`: no false match). The six entries of the word are scanned: an entry with the same `chk` and a nonzero length is a MATCH (two trails ended at this DP): the pair `(id, len)` of both trails goes to a queue of 4096 pairs, and the stored entry is replaced only if the new trail is longer (keep-longer, dkuthoore `0b2616e3`); otherwise the DP is written to an empty entry of the word, or, if the word is full, to the entry selected by a rotating counter. The lane receives a new trail (next id, `insert_lane`). A trail older than 1280 iterations is cut by a scan every 512 iterations.
5. Stage 2 (`Attack.stage2`) when the queue holds 3840 pairs and at the end of the window: pairs run in lane pairs `(p, p + 128)`, 128 slots, continuous refill; the longer trail starts first and the shorter `len` difference iterations later (timing wheel of 2048 buckets); after each iteration the two states are compared (12 planes, then the other 17 for candidates); the first equal state gives the predecessors `(x, y)`; `x = y` (the shorter trail started on the longer one) is discarded (`degenerate`).
6. The claw (`Attack.claw`, `claw_core`, every operation counted): sides from bit 28 (same side: discarded, `same`); decode `(a0, j)` and `u`; `F(a0)`, `P(u)`, the exact test of Lemma 2 (`notclaw` on failure); `a1 = u - F + a0 + K2` (the claw equation); `C1` on `W8x, W8y`; `C2` on `W9`; `G`; `F7` (the words from the closed forms of the step equations, asserted against `variantW` in check mode); step 3b: both second blocks `W0..W15` from `(cv, a0, a1)`, two 38-step compressions (2 x 2728 charged), exact comparison of the eight words. On acceptance the two 3-block digests are recomputed (6 x 2728 + 1600 charged) and the messages are output.

The attack is the loop over first blocks (`--attack`): the 16 words of each window's first block are drawn from the operating system's random source (fresh algorithmic coins; `--seed` exists only for the measurements and experiments). Halts: a success, the window count `B_WINDOWS`, the charged-work budget `W_BUDGET` (Section 7), and a window ends when its trail counter `idc` reaches `2^20` (it peaks at 526,959 over the 24 windows).

## 4. Machine model and prices (declared once)

Counted 256-bit word RAM with 512 registers allocated (16,384 bytes, charged in Section 6; the kernel uses at most 240), cost model `collision-frontier-v5`, unit the target compression = 2728 operations (`C` of `sha256-r38`). One operation each: XOR, AND, OR, NOT, move, shift, rotate, add, subtract, compare-and-set; a conditional branch is a compare and a branch (2); a load or a store is 3 (index shift, base add, access); a call and its return are 2. A kernel record (register-to-register XOR/AND/OR/NOT of 256-bit words) is one operation and is stored in 5 bytes (opcode and three 10-bit register fields; the price of `6b678689`, `0c1ef7c3`, `19034ce3`, `0b2616e3`). Every word operation of the program passes through the class `Mach` (one method = one charged primitive; `m.o` counts them); the kernel is charged `len(records)` per iteration; the program's own control flow that is not a `Mach` call (the six-entry scan loop and its flag tests, the keep-longer chain, the per-iteration cut-scan and halt tests, the initialisation of a stage-2 phase) is charged by explicit allowances in `m.o`: `CF_HIT = 16` per DP hit (an instrumented count found 14.3-15.7 decisions), `CF_ITER = 4` per iteration, `CF_PHASE = 1024` per stage-2 phase; the generator is charged the cap `GCAP = 2^21` per window (its own builder-call census, 2,870 calls at most over the 24 windows, each a handful of Python operations, is far below the cap; the cap is the charge). Memory is the inventory of Section 6, with the code charged as the full byte length of the shipped program. These are prices of the cost model, not probabilistic premises.

## 7. Success probability and time

`p_claw = q_pf x q3r = 2^-87.864031` with the charged `q_pf = 2^-16.463003` (Section 5) and the charged `q3r = 2^-71.4010281` (the inherited value of `51613d1c`, below our measurement `2^-71.1938777` of Section 7a; Section 9 for the premises).

**Claws are counted by groups.** Two claws of a window can share a trial only through a shared endpoint (`a0` or `u`); the distinct claws of a window are grouped into connected components by shared endpoint (`count_groups`), one claw per group is counted, and distinct groups have disjoint inputs `(a0, a1)`, hence distinct second blocks.

**Dependence between the groups of one window is bounded, not assumed away (premise H3).** Let `J_w` be the number of accepted groups of window `w` and `n_w` its number of groups. Premise P4 below gives `E[J_w | n_w] >= n_w p_claw`. Premise H3 bounds the dependence between groups that share a chaining value: `E[J_w (J_w - 1) | n_w] <= kappa E[J_w | n_w]` with `kappa = 2^-7.5`; that is, given that one group of a window is accepted, the expected number of OTHER accepted groups of the same window is at most `2^-7.5`. Independent groups give `(n_w - 1) p_claw = 2^-72.7`; at the measured 36,279 groups per window the premise still allows a pairwise co-acceptance rate up to `2^65.2` times the independent product. (The form is the consequence `E[J(J-1)] <= 2^-7.5 E[J]` of `51613d1c`'s H3-P1.) By the second-moment inequality `P(J >= 1) >= E[J]^2 / E[J^2] = E[J] / (1 + E[J(J-1)] / E[J])`, `P(J_w >= 1 | n_w) >= n_w p_claw / (1 + kappa)`. The windows are independent (P1), so, conditional on at least `G` groups in all, the run succeeds with probability at least `1 - prod_w (1 - n_w p_claw / (1 + kappa)) >= 1 - exp(-G p_claw / (1 + kappa))`. **The factor is charged:** set `G p_claw / (1 + 2^-7.5) = lambda = -ln(0.60999) = 0.494313`, so that `1 - exp(-lambda) = 0.390010` and `log2 G = 86.855474` (the factor costs 0.0079 bit; `p_claw` is computed from the charged `q_pf` with the unrounded bounds 0.0155384 (G) and 0.0576270 (F7), the exact rational `P(C1 C2)` and the unrounded `q3r`). The evidence for H3 at the stages this scale can observe, organizer-run and participant-run, is in Appendix E; its transfer to the deep event is the premise. With the lower bound `g- = 29,834.81` groups per window and the margin `(1 + 2^-19)`,
`B = ceil(G (1 + 2^-19) / g-) = 4692245158305164388716 = 2^71.9908` windows (first blocks), where `g- = 29,834.81` is the Ville 99% lower bound of Section 5; `B` is the integer the shipped program holds (`B_WINDOWS`), computed from the unrounded chain; the printed figures reproduce it to within 10^-5 relative (`log2 T` is unchanged at 4 decimals).

**Total work (Bernstein) and total groups (Hoeffding).** The windows are independent trials (fresh coins per window, H1 part (a)), each with charged work `W_j in [0, R]` and distinct groups in `[0, 2^25]`; `E[W_j] = mu <= mu+` (the declared premise) and `E[groups] = g >= g-` (the Ville bound's target) are premise H1 part (b). Since `0 <= W_j <= R`, `Var(W_j) <= E[W_j^2] <= R mu <= R mu+`, and Bernstein's inequality with `t = 2^-20 B mu+` gives: the total work of `B` windows exceeds `B mu+ (1 + 2^-20)` with probability at most `exp(-t^2 / (2 (B R mu+ + R t / 3))) = exp(-2^-40 B mu+ / (2 R (1 + 2^-20/3))) = exp(-2^7.25)` (the leader's Lemma 3 form; a plain Hoeffding bound with `R = 2^46` would be vacuous at this `B`). By Hoeffding's inequality the total number of distinct groups falls below `B g- (1 - 2^-20) >= G` with probability at most `exp(-2 B (g- 2^-20)^2 / 2^50) = exp(-2^12.72)`.

**The budget.** `--attack` stops at a success, after `B_WINDOWS = B` windows, or as soon as its charged work reaches `W_BUDGET = floor(B mu+ (1 + 2^-20)) + 1 = 3032045066544224755551741620595` operations (with the unrounded `mu+ = 646,181,534.1418878`, the Cantelli figure of Section 5) (checked before each window, 6 operations per window), whichever comes first; so its charged work is at most `W_BUDGET + R + 6 B` in every run. The time is
`T = (W_BUDGET + R + 6 B) / 2728 + 2^78.09276` target-compressions (the second term is the preprocessing allowance of Section 9, 0.0290% of the total), **`log2 T = 89.84492`, `time_log2 = 89.8450`** (rounded up; the plain means give 89.5415).

**What the 0.39 is conditional on (F-PROB-EPISTEMIC-SEPARATION).** The success probability is a statement about the fixed attack's own coins (the first blocks; nothing else is random). By the union bound it is at least `0.390010 - exp(-2^7.25) - exp(-2^12.72) > 0.39` **conditional on five premises about the fixed target and program**: (P1) windows are independent trials (H1(a)); (P2) the population mean of the operations per window is at most `mu+ = 646,181,534` (a declared heuristic premise, Section 5); (P3) the population mean of distinct groups per window is at least `g- = 29,834.81` (H1(b)); (P4) a distinct group is accepted with probability at least `p_claw = 2^-87.864031`: the exact C1 and C2 counts of `51613d1c` (H1-P1), G and F7 at least their charged rates, `q3r >= 2^-71.4010` on this walk's good pairs (H2-P1-q3-transfer, corroborated by run O); (P5) the within-window dependence bound `kappa <= 2^-7.5` (H3). The confidence statements by which P3 and the G and F7 rates were chosen (each one-sided 99%: Ville for `g-`, Wilson for G, cluster-robust for F7) are epistemic: they describe how the charged values were taken from finite data; they are not events of the attack's probability space and are not multiplied into the 0.39. ## 7a. The deep acceptance rate q3r: the inherited charge, and our registered measurement on this walk's population (run O, corroborating)

The per-seed data is attached (`experiments/q3data.py`, experiment `r38-q3-deep-rate-data`), and the leader's own records behind the inherited rate are quoted in Appendix C.

**Quantity.** `q3r(s) = Pr[ the eight output words after step 37 of the two members are equal | a good pair whose row-16 pass lies in subset s's 32 passes ]` under this program's good-pair law: `a0` uniform in `R = 9` given G, `a1` uniform over C1 and C2, `W6` uniform, `W7` uniform in F7, and `u = a1 + h(a0, cv)` in the walk's set (`u >> 28 = mix4(u mod 2^28)`). **Tool and estimator.** `q3walk.c` (own C code, sha256 `a2b67714dbea10267a8925f7bf4c1ffd91d4cde27ed9b322b21934a3157cea2a`, 20,473 bytes) re-implements the leader's sequential Monte Carlo:
- row 16 exactly (the subset's 32 passes);
- rows 17..21 driven by `E17x..E21x` in the characteristic's cells, with the cell measure;
- `N_P = 32768` particles, 256 proposals per particle and row, multinomial resampling of equally weighted particles;
- then 8192 late-word redraws per particle, which gives `2^28` window samples of rows 22..37. Every sample is two real 38-step traces rebuilt from message words. Replay mismatches are counted as diagnostics, and all are 0.

The organizer does not run the tool. A seed takes 28-56 s on four threads, against a 20 s sandbox. Intake admits only declared Python experiment sources (64 KiB in all), so the C source sits in the participant evidence bundle with the raw per-seed JSONs and is not in this package.

Per seed the estimate is `L = prefix_log2 + log2(EXACT / SAMPLES)`, and per row-16 pass `Lr = L + 27`. `2^Lr` is the product-of-stage-means estimator of a sequential Monte Carlo with multinomial resampling, so it is nonnegative and unbiased for `q3r(s)`. In the u1 arm the `u` condition makes the window factor a ratio over about 2^24 accepted samples, with relative bias O(2^-24). A pure-Python mirror that draws from the same counter-indexed streams matches the tool bit-for-bit on eight small instances (every field and 395 dumped samples).

## 9. Premises (declared in `claim.json`), the evidence for each in this package, and what remains premise

- **H1-walk-model** (score-critical). (a) The chaining value of a random first block is an independent random value for the maps `F` and the later events, so windows are independent trials. ## Appendix E. Dependence between the groups of one window (H3)

**E.1 What can be dependent.** Two distinct groups of one window have disjoint inputs `(a0, a1)` (groups are the connected components of shared `a0` or `u`, and `a1` is a function of `(a0, u)`), so their two second blocks differ; they share only the chaining value `cv`. The post-filters read `a1` (C1, C2), `(a0, a1)` (G) and `(a0, a1, cv[0])` (F7), and the deep event reads the whole second block and `cv`. Premise H3 (Section 7) bounds the expected number of other accepted groups of a window, given one, by `2^-7.5`, and its factor is charged; independence would give `2^-72.7`, so the premise tolerates a pairwise co-acceptance lift up to `2^65.2`. The statistics below measure that lift where it can be observed.

**E.2 Organizer-run (`r38-within-cv`; groups as in the attack).** 8 windows of 2^20 evaluations on the organizer seeds, 1,474 groups in all (184.2 per window); C1: 168 passes, co-pass lift 0.821 at the exact rate 1/8 (0.987 at the pooled rate), excess co-passes -4.53, X2 6.46 on 8 df; C1 C2: 9 passes, lift 0.145 (pooled 0.593), excess co-passes -3.94, X2 6.93 on 8 df; G 0 and F7 0 passes (too few at this scale for a dependence statistic). Per window (groups, C1, C1C2): 184, 26, 1; 184, 23, 2; 202, 17, 1; 172, 20, 0; 172, 15, 2; 199, 26, 0; 166, 18, 1; 195, 23, 2.
