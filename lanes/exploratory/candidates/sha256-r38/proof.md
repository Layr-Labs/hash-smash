# sha256-r38-exploratory: exact finite 39-percent collision construction

## 1. Target and complete declaration

The target is sha256-r38-prefix-v1, complete byte-string hashing with the
selected 38 prefix rounds and all 256 output bits. Standard IV,
message encoding, padding, feed-forward/tree construction and output
semantics remain intact. The algorithm returns two distinct legal
64-byte messages with equal complete target digests, or explicit failure.
Its success probability is strictly above 0.39; total cold computation
is below 2^133 target-compression units and peak memory below
2^137 bytes. Preprocessing is included and bounded by 2^133;
searched advice is zero. No candidate execution, experiment, actual
collision, quantum method, historical novelty or frontier improvement
is claimed. These are analytical bounds in the protected 256-bit word RAM.

## 2. Exact message handling and target normalization

Draw two independent uniform 256-bit words per sample, encode each as
32 little-endian bytes and concatenate. This is a uniform sample from
all 64-byte strings, a legal domain D of size 2^512. It is an internal
choice of messages, not a redefinition of the complete target.

Use exactly the standard fixed SHA-256 IV. For a 64-byte message, append 0x80, then 55 zero bytes, then the eight-byte big-endian original bit length 512. This gives two 512-bit compression blocks. Run prefix rounds 0..37, the first 38 schedule words and their original constants, on BOTH blocks; after each compression add every working state word to its incoming chaining word modulo2^32. Serialize all eight final chaining words big-endian. The ordinary complete hash requires two selected compression calls. Equal intermediate state or free-start behavior is never substituted for final digest equality.

There are exactly TWO compressions per sampled 64-byte message, and four for final verification of two messages.

The pinned cost-model table and LaneTrack.reference_operation_cost prescribe C=2728 for sha256-r38. The unchanged Word instrumentation in scripts/reference_operation_costs.py and verifier/hash_functions.py counts 30 operations per expanded schedule word, 54 per round and 16 feed-forward operations: 30*(38-16)+54*38+16=2728. Each narrow rotation has an input mask, two shifts, OR and final mask (five operations). A small sigma costs 13, each schedule sum costs four; the two round half-groups cost 26 and 24, with four state-update operations. This is the prescribed data-path normalization, not measured cycles; wrapper work is charged separately.

## 3. Fixed-budget construction

Set t=2^128. Allocate two arrays A and Z, each of t records of four
256-bit words: (full_digest, first_input_word, second_input_word, padding).
Each record is exactly 128 bytes. Initialize the two arrays explicitly.

For i=0,...,t-1, draw the two independent random words, form the 64-byte
message, compute its complete target hash and store its record in A.
Encode the 32 digest bytes as one 256-bit sorting key consistently;
this conversion is bijective, so equality compares every digest bit.
The original input words are retained, not merely their digest or seed.

Use bottom-up merge sort by the entire digest with the second array
as scratch. There are exactly 128 passes. Traverse equal-digest runs
linearly, holding the first input tuple of each run as its anchor.
If another tuple in that run is identical, skip that duplicate. If it
differs, retain the two messages. Every run containing at least two
distinct messages contains one different from its anchor, so the scan
does not miss a genuine collision in the table. At most one candidate
pair is sent to the final step.

Reconstruct the two canonical byte strings, verify inequality, recompute
both COMPLETE hashes and compare all 256 bits. Return the pair on
success. Otherwise return failure; no restart is performed. If no
distinct-input run was found, return failure after the scan. In an
error-free mathematical execution, a true table hit passes the final
check. The fixed budget also covers the explicit failure path.

All loops are bounded in advance. There is no uncharged repeated trial,
expected waiting time, statistical stopping rule, or seed-expanded
randomness. No collision pair, toy execution or cryptanalytic experiment
was run or supplied; the construction is analytical evidence.

## 4. Finite success without a uniform-output assumption

Let Q=2^256 denote the number of possible complete output strings.
For independent uniform U_i in D, the outputs Y_i=H(U_i) are independent
draws from some probability vector q=(q_1,...,q_Q). We do NOT assume
this vector is uniform, balanced, cryptographically pseudorandom or
generated by a random oracle. It is fixed by the actual target H.

For t<=Q, the probability all outputs differ is exactly t! e_t(q),
where e_t is the elementary symmetric polynomial of degree t. We prove
e_t(q)<=binom(Q,t)/Q^t directly. A maximum exists on the compact finite
probability simplex. Among maxima choose one minimizing sum(q_j^2).
For two coordinates u,v with sum a fixed, expand

    e_t(q)=E_t+a E_(t-1)+uv E_(t-2),

where E_k are elementary symmetric polynomials in the other coordinates,
E_0=1 and out-of-range terms are zero. E_(t-2)>=0. Replacing unequal
u,v by a/2,a/2 cannot decrease e_t. At a maximum it must preserve its
value, but strictly reduces u^2+v^2, a contradiction. Thus the selected
maximizer is uniform, and the displayed inequality follows. Zeros in
the vector cause no exception. Compactness is a mathematical proof,
not a nonuniform circuit or advice construction used by the algorithm.

Therefore

    Pr(no equal outputs) <= (Q)_t/Q^t
        = product_(j=0)^(t-1)(1-j/Q)
        <= exp(-t(t-1)/(2Q)).

For t=2^128, t(t-1)/(2Q)=1/2-2^-129>499/1000.
The positive exponential series gives

    exp(499/1000) > 1+499/1000+(499/1000)^2/2+(499/1000)^3/6
                 =9865254499/6000000000 > 1000/609.

The last strict inequality is exact: 9865254499*609=6007939989891,
larger than 6000000000000. Therefore the chance of equal outputs is
strictly greater than 391/1000. This event alone can count duplicate inputs.
For the 64-byte input domain N=2^512, a union bound gives

    Pr(any identical sampled inputs)<=binom(t,2)/N<2^-257.

Equal outputs with no repeated input yield a genuine distinct-message
collision found by the scan. Without independence of these two events,

    Pr(success)>391/1000-2^-257>39/100.

Thus the declared success_probability=0.39 is supported for the actual
fixed target. This reduces the earlier half-success sample budget by a
factor two; no earlier favorable review is a premise. There is no output
uniformity, ideal-hash, random-oracle, empirical or asymptotic assumption.
All t samples, failures and final verification occur within the fixed budget.

## 5. Complete primitive-work accounting

All addresses, counters and records use the protected 256-bit words.
The total table region below 2^137 bytes and the t=2^128 counter fit in
one word. No uncharged arbitrary-width address or distributed memory
oracle is introduced. The hypothetical RAM is not claimed to be
available physical hardware.

Reserve 8192 ordinary word operations per sample for the complete wrapper.
The following is finite analytical pseudocode, not an executed experiment.
A byte may occupy one 256-bit temporary cell; this increases fixed scratch
space only, which remains far below the separately reserved 1 MiB.
Each bounded loop charges its load/store, shifts/masks, pointer/counter
updates, comparison and branch, rather than inferring work from storage size.

1. Clear a 128-cell padded-byte buffer: 128 iterations, at most 8
   operations each (1024). For each of the 64 input bytes, extract the
   appropriate byte of one retained input word and store it in the first
   block: 64 iterations at most 16 each (1024).
2. Set the marker byte and eight length bytes of the second block: at
   most 9 explicit writes with address/value preparation, 8 each (72).
   All 55 intermediate zero bytes are already initialized.
3. Assemble each of 32 big-endian input words from four bytes. Initialize
   one accumulator per word (32*4), then visit each of the 128 bytes
   exactly once, shifting/ORing into that accumulator, at most 16 per
   visit (2048). This group is at most 2176.
4. Initialize eight chaining words, bind each of the two compression
   arguments and transfer resulting chaining words. At most 64 bounded
   word transfers/control steps at 8 operations each (512). Invoke the
   two separately priced compressions, retaining their feed-forward.
5. Serialize the eight final words to exactly 32 big-endian bytes, then
   assemble the complete digest key: 32 visits at most 16 each (512),
   plus 32 visits at most 8 each (256).
The five groups total 1024+1024+72+2176+512+512+256=5576 operations.
They describe each traversal once; there is no implicit byte-copy loop.

Every sample also draws and retains two independent random words (at most
16 operations), stores its four-word table record and updates its sample
counter (at most 256 operations). A separate 512-operation reserve covers
fixed initializations, call argument handling and control not in the loops.
The displayed target-specific sum plus 16+256+512 is below 8192.
Internal compression/permutation rounds are in the separate primitive calls.

Reserve 512t more word operations for initializing both arrays, including
all eight stored words per sample across the two buffers. No uninitialized
record is ever read, and zeroing time is not omitted.

For sorting, one record copy requires four loads, four stores, address
updates and fixed control within sixteen word operations. One digest
comparison requires two full-word loads, comparison, branches and
addresses within sixteen more. Each merge pass uses at most t comparisons,
2t record copies, and 128t additional index/end/tail bookkeeping operations.
Bound the pass by 256t plus 4096 fixed operations. For 128 passes,

    R_sort<=32768t+128*4096.

Reserve 512t operations for the final scan, full two-word input equality,
anchor changes, and remaining per-record checks. Reserve a fixed 2^22
ordinary operations for code/public-constant installation, array bases,
scratch initialization, final two-message serialization, inequalities,
output comparison and fixed success/failure handling. Final verification
also incurs at most FOUR additional target primitive calls. The fixed
ordinary allowance is separate from those calls.

Let P_calls be the total selected compression/permutation calls and
R_other all other priced word work. The NONOVERLAPPING bounds are

    P_calls <= 2t+4,
    R_other <= (8192+512+32768+512)t +128*4096+2^22
             =41984t+128*4096+2^22.

The exact normalization C=2728 is greater than 2048. Thus

    T=P_calls+R_other/C
     <(2+41984/2048)t+4+(128*4096+2^22)/2048
     =22.5t+2308 <32t=2^133.

The finite additive term is covered since t=2^128. The selected first
38 rounds execute on both padded blocks; all full-message work is priced.

    M<=2^136+2^20<2^137 bytes.
