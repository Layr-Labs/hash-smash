1. WHAT IS BEING ESTIMATED

q3c is the probability that a good pair of the submitted variant list closes rows 16..36 of
both members under every cell and every printed two-bit condition.  It enters the ledger only
through nfb = ceil(XREQ / (g * 32 * q3c)), so 0.01 bit of q3c is 0.01 bit of claim.

The organiser's dossier on our scored package raises the same defect four times
(F-COST-02, F-CRYPT-02, F-EXP-02, F-EXP-04), of which the two material forms are:

  * a family-transfer argument: an aggregate tail mean within 0.001 bit of uniform is used to
    bound the RETAINED sub-pool (lists 0, 1, 5, 6) within 0.002 bit.  Closeness of an
    aggregate does not bound a subgroup mean;
  * the declared q3c was adopted after the preregistered rule had fixed something else.

This block answers both by measuring the submitted pool directly: nothing is transferred,
and the rule below is fixed before the first seed runs.  It also tests, as a checkable
claim rather than an assertion, that the pool the estimator draws from is THIS filing's
list -- 599,760 words, the 170 classes of lists 0, 1, 5 and 6 -- and not the parents'
782,800-word pool.

4. STATISTIC AND DECISION RULE (fixed now, applied once)

For replicate i let x_i be the value printed on its RESULT line as `log2est` (log2 of the
linear SMC estimate; this is the convention of Appendix G.2 and G.4, and it is the convention
the declared q3c has always used).

  mean = sum x_i / n,  sd = the sample standard deviation (n-1 denominator),  n = 16384
  LB   = mean - t(0.99, n-1) * sd / sqrt(n)          one-sided 99% Student lower bound
  x_declared = floor(10000 * LB) / 10000             rounded DOWN (more negative), never up

  declared q3c = 2^x_declared, unless x_declared > -71.4355, in which case the declared
  value REMAINS -71.4355 (this block may only make the claim more conservative, never less).

Reported alongside, and NOT used in the claim unless the rule above yields it:
  the pooled bound of this block together with the 1024 replicates of Appendix G.4
  (n = 17408, same statistic, reported as a sensitivity).

5. WHAT WOULD REFUTE WHAT

  * If the mean lands materially above -71.42391 (the G.4 mean) the difference is not a
    property of the estimator's configuration but of the pool, and the pool hashes in
    section 2 are then the thing to check first.
  * If the pool hashes in section 2 do not reproduce from the shipped files, this filing's
    claim that the study measured its own list is refuted, and the correct action is not to
    re-run with a different pool but to correct the text.
  * Nothing in this block can make q3c larger; a worse outcome costs an iteration, not
    credibility.

6. WHY THIS AND NOT A LEDGER CUT

Iteration 31's own retraction (reports/iter31b-r37-gather-retraction.md) records that the
fused-gather price cut is not a saving once the run label has to be recovered.  A statistical
tightening of a quantity the ledger already uses at first order is the remaining lever that
needs no new cryptanalytic assumption.

Section 4 as first written made the wrong guard.  A larger q3c is a STRONGER claim, not a

pointed the wrong way and would have forbidden exactly the measurement this block exists to
make.  The rule is restated below, with no branch that depends on the outcome.

4'.  STATISTIC AND DECISION RULE (fixed now, applied once, no discretion)

For replicate i let x_i be `log2est` printed on its RESULT line.

  LB_pooled = mean(x) - t(0.99, n-1) * sd(x) / sqrt(n)
  over the union of block E (16384 seeds, this prereg) and appendix G.4's block
  (1024 seeds, 760000..761023, mean -71.42391, sd 0.15878) -> n = 17408.
  Same pool, same estimator, same configuration, each seed run exactly once.
  sd uses the n-1 denominator.  t is the Student 99% quantile.

  x_declared = floor(10000 * LB_pooled) / 10000        (rounded DOWN, more negative, never up)
  declared q3c = 2^x_declared.

This is applied as written whatever it yields:
  * if x_declared > -71.4355 the filing's claim improves and is filed;
  * if x_declared <= -71.4355 the current declaration -71.4355 stands, no new claim is filed,
    and block E is reported as evidence that the existing declaration is not too generous.

Pooling is used rather than "the better of the two blocks" precisely because taking the better
of two bounds is a selection over looks and would not be a 99% bound at all.

Also reported, and not used for any claim: block E alone (n = 16384, same statistic).
