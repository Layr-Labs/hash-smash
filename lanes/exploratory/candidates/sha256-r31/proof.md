# SHA-256, first 31 steps: improved two-block collision

## 1. Target and score

Track sha256-r31-exploratory, profile sha256-r31-prefix-v1, cost model
collision-frontier-v5. The object is an ordinary collision of the complete
reduced hash: FIPS 180-4 IV, standard length padding, feed-forward after
every block, original steps 0 through 30, and all 256 output bits.

The submitted scalar is time_log2 = 50.0. Peak memory is bounded by 2^54
bytes. Success probability used for the bound is 0.5. A checked witness is
attached so the organizer implementation can confirm the target. Replaying
the witness is not the scored search.

## 2. Algorithm

The procedure is the two-block conversion of a 31-step semi-free-start
characteristic into an ordinary collision, as improved by Li, Liu, and Wang
in the EUROCRYPT 2024 record (ePrint 2024/349, Section 4.2). Nonzero
message differences are confined to the seven expanded words

    W5, W6, W7, W8, W9, W16, W18.

The first five expanded words have no difference and no condition, so they
can connect the standard IV to the characteristic. The phases are:

1. Build one starting point: values of (A_i) for 1 <= i <= 12, (E_i) for
   5 <= i <= 12, and (W_i) for 9 <= i <= 12 that satisfy the differential
   conditions on steps 5 through 12. The paper measures this model solve at
   about 2^31.7 compressions. One starting point is enough for the bound
   below.

2. From that starting point, enumerate the free words that preserve the local
   collision in the expansion. The paper counts 2^14, 2^23, 2^27, and 2^25
   admissible values of W5, W6, W7, and W8 before the E3 and E4 conditions.
   Experiments reported there leave 2^11 valid (W7, W8) pairs. The product
   is 2^14 * 2^23 * 2^11 = 2^48 solutions of the early state and message
   words. Store them. This list is the memory term.

3. Compute first message blocks from the standard IV and match their chaining
   words (A_{-3}, A_{-2}, A_{-1}) against the list. A match determines
   W0 through W4 and E0.

4. Use the freedom in W13, W14, and W15 to finish the uncontrolled conditions
   on E13, E14, E15, W16, and W18. The paper's 100-test estimate is that this
   step succeeds with probability about 2^{-1.3}. On failure, take another
   first block.

5. Pad both complete messages with ordinary SHA-256 padding. Equal lengths
   make the padding block identical, so a collision of the chaining value is
   a collision of the full digest.

Failed trials and the final rehash are inside the time bound. The
characteristic is regenerated by the model in phase 1; it is not free advice.

## 3. Cost arithmetic

Section 4.2 writes the two-block cost, before the new characteristic, as

    2^{96 - ell + gamma} + 2^ell * T_tool,

where ell is the log of the stored solution list and gamma is the negative
log of the phase-4 success probability. For the new characteristic, with one
starting point, ell = 48 and gamma is about 1.3, and the model cost is
negligible beside the first term. The paper therefore estimates

    2^{96 - 48 + 1.3} + 2^{48} = 2^{49.3} + 2^{48} = about 2^{49.8}.

The same arithmetic in this note:

    2^{49.3} + 2^{48} = 2^{48} * (2^{1.3} + 1) = 2^{48} * 3.4623 < 2^{49.80}.

Under collision-frontier-v5, one reduced compression is one unit. Word
operations outside a compression cost 1/2140 unit. Even a surcharge of 1024
word operations per trial is less than half a unit, which lifts 2^{49.8} by
less than 2^{48.8} and stays below 2^{50}. The submitted time_log2 is 50.0.

The 2^{31.7} starting-point solve is charged as preprocessing and is dominated
by the online term. Memory is 2^{48} records. At 64 bytes per record that is
2^{54} bytes, which is the reported memory bound. Memory is not part of the
scalar.

A search whose expected cost is 2^{49.8} and whose budget is 2^{50} has
success probability greater than 0.5 under the usual independent-trial model.
The claim records 0.5.

## 4. Witness

The certificate messages are two distinct 128-byte strings. Under the
organizer function digest(message, "sha256", 31) both evaluate to

    55fdfb37efcbd086e19c3de0f72596300a3acdf48da5b1d0450a592bb2869fcd.

The same call at 32 steps does not collide. The messages share the first
block and differ in the second, which is the two-block shape, including the
organizer's extra padding block.

The witness is a published example of a later practical 31-step collision.
It shows that the organizer's IV, padding, step index, and 256-bit comparison
agree with an ordinary 31-step collision. It does not reduce the charged cost,
and it is not a transcript of the 2^{49.8} search.

## 5. What was not claimed

This package does not claim a 32-step collision, a free-start collision, or a
complexity below 2^{49.8}. Later practical 31-step attacks exist and would
improve the scalar if their condition counts were re-derived here. They are
not required to justify this ceiling. The nominal reference label
sha256-r31-nominal-v2 is schema metadata. The scalar 50 is below the promoted
public best of 136. Readiness requests review. It does not assert that a
human has accepted the candidate or that Yukon has promoted it.
