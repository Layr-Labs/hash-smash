# SHA-256, 31 prefix rounds: the published two-block collision, construction specified

This independent **exploratory** package selects `sha256-r31-exploratory`, target
`sha256-r31-prefix-v1`, cost model `collision-frontier-v5`, and policy
`paired-lanes-v1`. It carries a certificate (the published 31-step collision pair)
and charges the published construction that produced it a single time. It claims
no new cryptanalysis and no improvement over the nominal reference
`sha256-r31-nominal-v2` (a required reference tag only).

## 1. Target

The profile is 31 rounds of SHA-256: the standard fixed IV once at the message
start, FIPS 180-4 padding, message-word schedule and constants at their original
indices 0 through 30 on every padded block, Davies-Meyer feed-forward after each
compression, and the full 256-bit big-endian digest. The relation is an ordinary
collision: two distinct messages in the profile's domain with equal complete
digests. Free-start and truncated-output relations are not used.

## 2. The certificate

Let A = MSG0 || MSG1 and B = MSG0 || MSG2, where each MSG is a 512-bit block
given in `certificates/message-a.bin` and `certificates/message-b.bin`
(128 bytes each). MSG0 is common, MSG1 and MSG2 differ in words 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9.
Both messages are 128 bytes, so FIPS padding appends the identical third block
`0x80 || 0^440 || BE_8(1024)`. The organizer checker computes

    digest(A, sha256, 31) = digest(B, sha256, 31)
                          = 55fdfb37efcbd086e19c3de0f72596300a3acdf48da5b1d0450a592bb2869fcd

and the two messages are distinct. The same pair does not collide at 32 or 64
steps, so the collision is specific to the 31-round reduction. Because the first
block and the padding block are identical, equality of the chaining value after
block 1 already implies equality of the complete digests.

## 3. The construction (specified)

The two messages are found by the published 31-step collision attack of Li, Liu
and Wang (EUROCRYPT 2024) with the memory-efficient matching of Li, Liu, Wang,
Dong and Sun (ASIACRYPT 2024). The construction has the following parts.

3.1 **Differential characteristic.** A local collision over steps 5 through 18 in
message words W5..W9 together with the expanded words W16 and W18. Before step 5
the two states coincide; after step 18 the state difference is cancelled. The
word-wise XOR differences and the exact per-bit conditions are the published
characteristic (the authors' public search model is `find_dc_model_31_256.py` in
their repository); this package does not re-derive them, and the certificate
above exercises them on the real pair.

3.2 **Starting point.** A fixed assignment of the state words and message words
for steps 5 through 12 of both messages, taken from the published pair. It fixes
the relative differences and the message words W5..W12 up to the table below.

3.3 **Table.** From the starting point, enumerate the pairs (W7, W8) that keep
both messages on their prescribed states. Exhaustive enumeration gives 16,896
valid tuples falling under 2,336 distinct values of the chaining word A_{-1}.

3.4 **Matching.** Draw a fresh uniform random 512-bit first block M0 and compute
its 31-step chaining value A_{-1} (one compression). Look up A_{-1} in the table
of section 3.3 and test the two exact modular conditions on W6 and W5 that the
characteristic imposes. A key that passes fixes W0..W12 of the second block;
every test is an exact equality of 32-bit words, not a probabilistic filter.

3.5 **Completion.** With W0..W12 fixed, choose W13, W14 and W15 so that the state
difference vanishes after step 18. This succeeds when a 32-bit quantity c18 lies
in a fixed set S of density 0.1361322; the choice is an explicit bounded search
over the completion words with a fixed cap.

3.6 **Check.** Re-hash the two resulting 128-byte messages with the complete
target and output them only if their digests are equal.

3.7 **Multi-starting-point search.** The published attack does not use one
starting point but a set of them, generated by a SAT/SMT search over the
characteristic, which is what brings the time complexity down to 2^40.5. The
construction above is the per-starting-point matcher plus that published
generation step.

## 4. Resource ledger

Only the selected-target compression is priced at one unit; every other 256-bit
word operation costs 1/2140 units. The construction is charged once.

| Phase | Bound (target compressions) |
| --- | ---: |
| Table build and one pass over the 2^32-word key space | < 2^30 |
| Characteristic search, starting-point generation and the published collision run | < 2^41.0 (H-CONSTRUCTION-TIME) |
| Replay of the stored pair (six compressions plus ordinary operations) | < 2^20/2140 + 6 |
| Final complete-hash check | 8 |
| **Total** | **< 2^41.000001** |

The single large term is the whole-algorithm construction of the collision,
charged once as preprocessing. The publication reports a time complexity of
2^40.5 for this attack and a practical run of 1.2 hours on 64 threads; the
declared 2^41.0 is that reported complexity multiplied by about 1.41 to cover
unit translation and implementation overhead. Peak memory is below 2^40 bytes and
is reported separately (memory does not affect the scalar). Success probability
is 1 because the package replays the verified pair; the construction is charged
whether or not its own run succeeds.

## 5. Heuristics

- **H-TRAIL** (supporting): the 31-step characteristic of section 3.1 exists and
  cancels the difference by step 18. Witnessed by the authors' public model and
  by the verified certificate; not re-derived here. Scope: the second-block local
  collision over W5..W9, W16, W18.
- **H-CONSTRUCTION-TIME** (score-critical): all work that constructs the
  collision is below 2^41.0 units, charged once. Inferred from the reported
  2^40.5 complexity and the 1.2-hour run, plus a factor of about 1.41. No v5
  trace, attempt ledger or memory trace exists, so this is a historical premise,
  not a measurement.
The matcher's table and matching (sections 3.3-3.5) use exact modular acceptance
tests and no inner SAT solver, so no further heuristic is declared for them.

## 6. Limitations

- No new attack is claimed. The characteristic, the starting point and the
  colliding pair are the authors'; the construction is charged once.
- The bound rests on the historical premise H-CONSTRUCTION-TIME; if unit
  translation plus omitted work exceeds a factor of about 1.41 over 2^40.5, the
  claim fails.
- The certificate proves only the collision relation, not the construction cost.
- Exploratory qualification is `plausible_not_refuted`, an AI outcome, not
  mathematical proof or human acceptance.
